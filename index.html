Stolac, Bosnia & Herzegovina

Discover Stolac & Herzegovina

Located in the Herzegovina-Neretva Canton of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Stolac serves as an essential cultural transit link connecting Sarajevo and Mostar with Trebinje, Dubrovnik, and Montenegro. Positioned along the Bregava river, the area features UNESCO-recognized cultural sites including the Radimlja stećci necropolis, the ancient Hellenistic city of Daorson, and the medieval fortress of Vidoški. Guesthouse Check In offers an ideal base for exploring Herzegovina's natural and historic landmarks.
