Stolac, Bosnia & Herzegovina

Discover Stolac

Located in the Herzegovina-Neretva Canton of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Stolac is a captivating town nestled in the picturesque region of Herzegovina. Positioned along the tourist route that traverses Herzegovina, it serves as a vital link connecting the mountainous Bosnian hinterland with the coastal regions of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Dubrovnik, and Montenegro. Situated in the Herzegovina Humina area, Stolac benefits from a strategic location on a route spanning from Sarajevo through Mostar, Stolac, Ljubinje, and Trebinje — reaching Dubrovnik in under 4 hours. Thanks to its rich hunting grounds and other natural advantages, Stolac and its surroundings have been inhabited since ancient times by prehistoric communities, the Illyrians, Romans, and Slavs, all of whom left behind a wealth of anthropological evidence.
